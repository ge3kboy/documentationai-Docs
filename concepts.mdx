## Uploader Journey

ScreenThis supports two primary user journeys — uploading and sharing video content, and viewing content as a guest. Each journey connects through the access code system.

Managers use the uploader flow to register videos, wait for processing, and send controlled invitations to guests. The workflow starts in the Content Library and ends when a viewer receives an invitation with access rules such as expiry and allowed views.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in" icon="log-in" title-type="p">

    Authenticate via Supabase Auth with your email and password.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a video" icon="upload" title-type="p">

    Navigate to Content Library and register a video. Processing is handled by Gumlet via automation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing" icon="clock" title-type="p">

    Video status transitions from pending to processing to ready. You will be notified when it is ready.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a viewer" icon="mail" title-type="p">

    Select a ready video, enter the guest email, set expiry and view limits, and send the invitation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage invitations" icon="settings" title-type="p">

    Review, resend, or revoke invitations from the Home dashboard.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewer (Guest) Journey

Guests move through a short, gated access flow designed to protect content without requiring a full account. Access depends on the invitation link, a six-character code, accepted terms, and a valid guest session issued by the server.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive invitation" icon="mail" title-type="p">

    Guest receives an email with a direct link and a six-character access code.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter access code" icon="key" title-type="p">

    Open the link or Guest Access page and enter the code.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept terms" icon="file-check" title-type="p">

    Review and accept the terms and conditions to unlock playback.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the video" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">

    The server verifies the code, issues a guest token, and opens the protected player with a watermark.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## How flows connect

The two journeys connect through the access code. Managers create codes and send invitations. Guests use codes to access videos. Each code carries expiry, view limits, and company-level isolation enforced by Row-Level Security.
