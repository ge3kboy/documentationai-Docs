## Administration tasks

Administrators manage people, company settings, communication, and data retention. This guide covers account management, manager and guest administration, company configuration, email settings, and retention policies.

## Account management

Use the profile menu in the top-right corner to manage your own account. These actions affect only your sign-in session and password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change password" icon="key" title-type="p">
    Select your profile image or initials, choose **Change Password**, enter a password with at least eight characters, confirm it, and select **Update Password**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log out" icon="log-out" title-type="p">
    Open the profile menu and select **Log out**. Finish or cancel any active upload first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage managers

Manage managers from **Settings**. Use this area to invite staff, resend setup emails, adjust optional access, and remove accounts when someone should no longer sign in.

### Invite a manager

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Invite Manager" icon="user-plus" title-type="p">
    Open **Settings** and find the manager section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter details" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Enter the manager's email address and assign a company.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send invite" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Select **Invite**. The manager receives an account setup email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Resend an invitation

Find the manager and use the resend action when the original setup email expired or was not received. Use this before creating a second invitation for the same person.

### Grant permissions

Adjust optional access from the manager's settings or permissions controls. Grant only the access the manager needs for their role.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Administrators can control Analytics access and Advance Invites access for each manager. Advance Invites must also be enabled for the manager's company.
</Callout>

### Remove a manager

Use the manager's action menu and confirm removal when the account should no longer be used. Removing a manager blocks future sign-in for that account.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Removing a manager prevents future sign-in but does not remove historical records of actions already taken.
</Callout>

## Manage guests

Open **Settings** and choose the guest section. **Guest Emails** lists addresses previously invited by the company.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Removing a guest can also remove that guest's active access codes for the company. Read the confirmation before continuing.
</Callout>

## Company settings

Open **Settings** and select a company. Company controls may include general details, feature permissions, video upload settings and allowed formats, video allocation and usage, email settings and templates, and manager permissions.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Changes to company settings can affect every manager and invitation in that company. Review the selected company before saving.
</Callout>

## Communication settings

Use communication settings to control how invitation and test emails are sent, formatted, tracked, and reviewed. Most changes in this area affect company-wide email behavior.

### Email delivery

Review delivery callback information and copy the displayed URLs when configuring the organization's email system. These settings are normally managed by a system administrator.

### Email templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose template" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Select **Global defaults** or **Company override**, then choose the template type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit message" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Edit the message using placeholders for guest-specific values.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and save" icon="check" title-type="p">
    Preview the result and select **Save**. Use **Restore defaults** to discard custom wording.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Email verification

Administrators can test an address and manage how Advance Invites verification works. Change these settings only when you understand the impact on batch review and sending.

## Data retention

Open **Settings** and select **Data Retention**. Retention actions can affect old invitations, viewing logs, audit logs, and email events.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose record type" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Select the record type and age for the retention action.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dry run" icon="eye" title-type="p">
    Select **Dry run** and review the number of records that would be affected.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run action" icon="trash-2" title-type="p">
    Complete a dry run first, then select the run, archive, or delete action and confirm the warning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Use retention tools only under your organization's approved data policy. Always complete a dry run first to verify the scope.
</Callout>
