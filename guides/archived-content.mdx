## Archived content keeps removed videos and records available for historical and compliance review.

<Callout kind="info">

Archived content is separated from the active Content Library. Archiving does not erase data — it moves it out of the default view so active lists stay focused.

</Callout>

## View archived videos

Navigate to **Archived** and open the **Videos** tab to see videos that have been archived. Each entry shows the title, archive date, and associated metadata.

## Access codes for archived videos

The **Access Codes** tab shows codes linked to archived videos. These codes are inactive but remain visible for audit and historical review.

<Callout kind="alert">

Access codes for archived videos are inactive. Guests cannot use them to access videos. To restore guest access, restore the video first and then create a new invitation.

</Callout>

## Viewing logs

The **Viewing Logs** tab shows historical guest viewing data for archived content. Use it for compliance review and historical analysis.

## Restore a video

Restore a video from the **Archived** section when you need to return it to the active library. Once restored, the video appears in normal active lists again.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Archived" icon="archive" title-type="p">

  Navigate to **Archived** and open the **Videos** tab.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the video" icon="search" title-type="p">

  Locate the archived video you want to restore.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Restore" icon="rotate-ccw" title-type="p">

  Select the restore action and confirm.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Restoration may be blocked if the company has no remaining video allocation. Contact your administrator to adjust quotas if needed.

</Callout>

## Review older records

Use the **Archived** tabs to review older invitations and viewing logs that no longer appear in active lists. Archive records are useful for historical and compliance review.
