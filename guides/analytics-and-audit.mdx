## Analytics at a glance

ScreenThis provides analytics tools for tracking viewing activity and engagement, with CSV export for compliance reporting.

## Analytics overview

Open Analytics to review summary metrics. The overview cards show total videos, total invitations, total guest views, unique guests, and views over time.

## Viewing logs

The Guest Viewing Logs table shows per-video view history. Each log entry includes the guest, video, date, device, and viewing information.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Guest Viewing Logs" icon="eye" title-type="p">

  Navigate to **Analytics** and open **Guest Viewing Logs**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort or filter" icon="filter" title-type="p">

  Use available controls to sort or filter results.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a log" icon="file-text" title-type="p">

  Select a log entry to open **Log Details** and review the guest, video, date, device, and viewing information.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export viewing activity

Export viewing activity after you confirm the correct scope and filters. This helps you download only the records you need for reporting or review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm company" icon="building" title-type="p">

  Verify the correct company is selected in the top bar.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">

  Apply the intended filters or date range.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Export CSV" icon="download" title-type="p">

  Select the CSV export action and save the downloaded file securely.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Exports can contain guest information and should be handled according to your organization's privacy rules.

</Callout>
