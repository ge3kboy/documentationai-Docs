## Manage videos in one workspace

The Content Library is your video management hub. Upload new content, search and filter existing videos, edit details, replace files, and archive content — all from one workspace.

## Find and display videos

Use the search icon to find videos by title. Toggle between list and grid layouts to view the library the way you prefer. Only active videos appear in the normal library, and archived videos are kept separately.

## Upload a video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Content Library" icon="folder" title-type="p">

  Navigate to **Content Library** and select **Video Upload**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a file" icon="upload" title-type="p">

  Select **Browse Files** and choose one supported video file.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter details" icon="edit" title-type="p">

  Enter a clear **Content Title** and optionally add a **Poster Image**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Start upload" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">

  Review the detected duration and file size, then select **Start Upload**. Keep the page open during upload.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Wait for processing to finish before inviting guests. The upload panel shows the current stage and percentage. Select **Cancel Upload** only when you intend to stop.

</Callout>

## Resume an interrupted upload

Resume an interrupted upload from the same upload panel in Content Library. Use the original local file so the saved upload session can be matched correctly.

<Callout kind="tip">

Return to **Content Library**, open **Video Upload**, and choose the same file. When **Resume upload available** appears, select **Resume Upload**. Use **Start Over** only to discard the saved session.

</Callout>

## Poster images

Poster controls let you upload from your device, select a stored company image, or use a direct image URL. Choose a clear image with enough resolution for library cards and invitation emails.

## Edit video details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open video actions" icon="settings" title-type="p">

  Open the video's actions menu.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Update details" icon="edit" title-type="p">

  Select **Video Details** and update the title, description, or poster.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Save" icon="check" title-type="p">

  Select the save action to confirm changes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite from a video card

Select **Invite** beside a ready video to send an invitation directly from the card. Enter the guest email, set views allowed, choose an expiration date, and select **Create & Send**.

## View access codes for a video

Open the video's codes action to see all invitations associated with that video. Use it to review active guest access without searching the full invitation history.

## Replace a video

Upload a replacement when the content file needs to change but the video should stay in the same business workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open replacement option" icon="edit" title-type="p">

  Open the video's actions and choose **Upload Replacement Video**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select replacement file" icon="upload" title-type="p">

  Choose the new file and review its details.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and upload" icon="check" title-type="p">

  Start the upload and confirm the replacement. Wait for processing to finish.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Unviewed invitations can move to the replacement. Invitations that have already been viewed remain with the archived version so prior activity stays accurate.

</Callout>

## Archive a video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Video Details" icon="file-text" title-type="p">

  Open the video you want to archive.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Archive" icon="archive" title-type="p">

  Select **Archive** and confirm the action.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Archiving removes the video from the active Content Library but does not immediately erase it. Active access codes for archived videos are inactive.

</Callout>
