## ScreenThis at a glance

ScreenThis is a secure video hosting and controlled-sharing platform for teams that need to deliver video to specific viewers under strict access controls. Upload videos, generate guest access codes, enforce expiry and view limits, apply dynamic watermarking and DRM protections, and track viewing activity across companies with tenant-level isolation.

## Why ScreenThis?

ScreenThis helps you share sensitive or time-limited video without handing out unrestricted links. Each invitation can be tied to a specific guest with its own access code, expiry date, view limit, watermarking, and company-scoped access rules, so you can control who watches, how often they watch, and how activity is recorded.
