## Quick checks

Use this guide when ScreenThis displays an error, an action is unavailable, or a guest cannot open a video. Work through the quick checks first, then find the section that matches your issue.

<Callout kind="tip">

Before investigating further:

1. Refresh the page once.
2. Confirm you are signed in with the correct email address.
3. Check that the video is ready and not archived.
4. Recheck guest email spelling, expiration, start time, timezone, and allowed views.
5. If an upload or batch is already running, use its Resume control instead of creating a duplicate.

</Callout>

## Sign-in issues

Most sign-in problems come from using the wrong invited email address, an incomplete invitation, or a password mismatch. Check the message you see, then use the matching fix below.

### Failed to sign in

Check that you are using the invited email address and the correct password. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you have never completed the invitation, return to the invitation email first. Ask an administrator to resend the invitation or confirm that your account is still active.

### Did not receive an account invitation

Check spam, junk, quarantine, and filtered email folders. Confirm the invited email with your administrator. Ask the administrator to resend the invitation. Ask your email administrator whether automated account messages are blocked.

### Invitation link expired or does not work

Ask an administrator to resend the manager invitation. Do not create a second account with another email unless your administrator intends to do so.

### Forgot password

The portal does not show a self-service reset link. Contact your administrator or support for account recovery.

### Password must be at least 8 characters

Choose a password with eight or more characters. Enter the same password in both fields to avoid the Passwords do not match error.

## Missing pages or permissions

Some areas of ScreenThis require additional access. Managers normally see Home and Content Library, plus any optional features granted to them. Contact your administrator if you need access to additional areas.

## Upload problems

Uploads can stall if the page closes, the session is interrupted, or the same file is started twice. Resume the existing upload instead of creating a second one.

### Upload fails or stalls

Keep the page open during upload. If interrupted, return to Content Library, open Video Upload, choose the same file, and select Resume Upload. Do not start a second upload of the same file. Select Cancel Upload only when you intend to stop.

## Invitation issues

Invitation problems usually come from an incorrect guest email, delivery filtering, or trying to resend a code that can no longer be used. Check the guest record and video status before sending again.

### Guest didn't receive the email

Check the guest email spelling. Check the invitation delivery status on Home. Resend the invitation. Ask the guest to check spam and junk folders. Confirm email verification settings with an administrator.

### Can't resend an invitation

Confirm the video is ready and not archived. Confirm you are in the correct company. If the code is expired or exhausted, use Reinvite instead of Resend to create a fresh access code.

## Access code problems

Access codes stop working when they expire, run out of views, or point to a video that is no longer available. Verify the code details before asking the guest to try again.

### Code not accepted by the guest

Confirm the code has not expired and views are not exhausted. Confirm the video is not archived. Check that the guest is entering the correct six-character code. Ask the guest to refresh the page and try again.

## Playback issues

Playback failures usually come from video status, browser conditions, or access timing. Start by confirming that the guest is trying to watch a ready video during its allowed viewing window.

### Video won't play

Confirm the video is ready. Confirm the guest accepted terms and conditions. Try a different browser. Check the internet connection. If the viewing window has not started, the guest must wait until the scheduled time.

## Still need help?

Use the in-app help option first so your request starts from the correct account context. Permission changes and account recovery usually need help from your administrator.

<Callout kind="info">

While signed in, select Get Help at the bottom of the left menu. On a phone or tablet, open the menu first. For account or permission issues, contact your administrator.

</Callout>
