## Recent updates

# ScreenThis Changelog

<Update label="2026-08-03" description="Guided sharing, stronger batch workflows, and a new Help Center" tags={["product","help-center"]}>
  ## New Help Center

  - Added a complete getting-started guide for account activation, first sign-in, uploading a first video, and sending a first invitation.
  - Added step-by-step walkthroughs for Home, Content Library, guest viewing, Analytics, Archived content, account administration, and Advance Invites.
  - Added troubleshooting and FAQs for account access, uploads, invitations, playback, permissions, and delivery issues.

  ## Easier video uploads and management

  - Upload progress is clearer from preparation through final processing.
  - Interrupted uploads can be resumed by selecting the same file again.
  - Quota warnings now explain when capacity is low or an upload cannot continue.
  - Replacement uploads preserve viewing history while moving eligible unused invitations to the updated video.

  ## More reliable guest invitations

  - Invitation forms verify email addresses and warn when an address needs attention.
  - Viewing windows support start time, expiration, timezone, and per-guest view allowances.
  - Invitation details make it easier to resend, reinvite, revoke access, and review delivery status.
  - Guests receive clearer messages when access has not started, has expired, has reached its view limit, or is temporarily rate-limited.

  ## Advance Invites workflow

  - Added a guided four-step batch workflow covering setup, email design, recipients, verification, review, and sending.
  - Added reusable email templates, previews, and test sends.
  - Recipient verification separates addresses into **Existing**, **Safe new**, and **Needs review** groups.
  - Batch controls support pause, resume, cancellation, retrying failed recipient sets, cloning, and reviewing prior batches.

  ## Visibility and control

  - Analytics and exports provide clearer insight into invitations and guest viewing.
  - Administrator tools cover archived content, audit history, company settings, people, and retention controls.
  - Role and company permissions keep optional areas such as Analytics and Advance Invites limited to authorized users.
</Update>
